# Phase 1A: Gameplay and market correctness audit

**Baseline:** `origin/main` at `a6c229e` (2026-10-18). **Scope:** read-only review of gameplay, pricing, orders, risk, career, scenarios, saves and their tests; this PR changes documentation only. P0 means a core invariant or progression is broken now; P1 means a materially wrong player-visible result; P2 means a narrower misleading or incomplete mechanic. Examples below use the shipped engine directly, not a broker or live market. No production or schema changes were made.

## Executive summary

Do **not** use equity, rank, margin or end-of-day results for competitive scoring yet. An unvalidated negative order quantity creates cash and an invalid position; a long's unrealized gain/loss is counted twice in net liquidation; simultaneous pending buys can borrow cash before margin unlock; closing a long can open a forbidden short. The only reputation-award method has no caller, so advancement beyond the first rank is unreachable through normal play. Bracket exits are independent orders: after one closes the position, the other remains live. Fix validation, accounting and order invariants before tuning realism or adding leaderboard verification.

## Verified behavior and coverage

- The engine seeds pricing, depth, events and rivals from one `SeededRNG`, starts with 48 equities and a scheduled earnings calendar, and exports/imports RNG, prices, portfolio, career, orders, rivals and queued earnings jumps (`src/engine/market.ts:129-205`). The UI drives `tick` every 50 ms, caps elapsed input at 0.5 seconds, and publishes snapshots at a speed-dependent cadence (`app/(game)/terminal/page.tsx:51-85`). `src/engine/engine.test.ts:203-252` compares seeded price/volume/direction paths and a save round trip, not the complete action log, timestamps or order lifecycle.
- Orders support market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing-stop; market/stop fills walk five synthetic depth levels, while limit fills are handled separately (`src/engine/matching.ts:68-181`, `src/engine/orderbook.ts:112-159`). Commission is charged on fills (`src/engine/portfolio.ts:188-190`); risk and daily financing exist (`src/engine/portfolio.ts:346-488`). A bounded-price/volatility property suite exists (`src/engine/pricing.property.test.ts`), but does not assert portfolio identities, OHLCV or account permissions.
- A save records portfolio, price candles, orders, simulation clock and `actionSeq`; player orders/skip flush locally and tick-driven day changes are persisted (`src/engine/market.ts:159-205`, `src/store/useTerminalStore.ts:273-319,406-439,478-487`). Changing a financial invariant must preserve old saves, not silently rewrite or discard them. Phase 0 replay/seq/cloud guarantees are in `docs/ROADMAP.md:65-96`.
- **Executed reproductions:** temporary TypeScript probes outside the repository (`tsx`) against this commit produced the concrete outputs below. Code-derived findings not independently exercised are labeled as such. No browser claim is made. TradingView documents that same-time updates replace the last bar and a later time creates a new bar ([Lightweight Charts 5.2 getting started](https://tradingview.github.io/lightweight-charts/docs#updating-the-data-in-a-series)). Monetary boundary values should be constructed from strings rather than pre-rounded JS arithmetic when changing the accounting, per [decimal.js's README](https://github.com/MikeMcl/decimal.js#readme).

## Bugs, ordered by priority

### G0 · P0 · Negative quantities mint cash and corrupt positions

**Where:** `src/engine/market.ts:219-246,253-277,392-407`; `src/engine/orderbook.ts:112-119`; `src/engine/portfolio.ts:173-224`; saved `quantity` is only `z.number()` at `src/saves/schema.ts:187-194`.
**Reproduce (executed):** `const m = new MarketSimulationEngine('invalid-qty',10000); m.placeOrder({ticker:'HLVG',side:'buy',type:'market',quantity:-10})`. **Expected:** reject a non-positive/non-integer/non-finite quantity without creating an order, trade or position. **Actual:** success; cash becomes **$11,515.80** and a **-10-share** position with *negative* market value appears. Book estimation silently clamps quantity to one, but portfolio applies the original -10 shares, subtracting a negative notional. Even though the numeric ticket clamps ordinary input (`src/features/ticket/OrderTicket.tsx:235-241`), the shared engine/store and deserialized state must not trust UI validation. **Impact:** fabricated cash and nonsensical exposure/equity; any later scoring/replay is meaningless. **Fix/test:** validate quantity and prices at the engine entry and imported order boundary, revalidate before any fill mutation; reject malformed legacy rows without deleting their raw backup. Table-test zero/negative/fractional/NaN/Infinity quantities, invalid limit/stop prices and normal positive fills.

### G1 · P0 · Equity includes unrealized P&L twice

**Where:** `src/engine/portfolio.ts:138-155,380-406,413-435,439-474`; consumer `src/engine/market.ts:373-389,457-515`.
**Reproduce (executed):** `new PortfolioEngine(10000)`; buy 10 HLVG at $100 with $0 commission via `executeFill`; `updateMarketPrices(new Map([['HLVG',110]]))`; call `calculateRiskMetrics(COMPANY_MAP)`. **Expected:** $9,000 cash + $1,100 marked long = **$10,100**. **Actual:** $10,200: marked value already contains the $100 gain, then `netLiquidationValue` adds `totalUnrealizedPnl` again. A falling price subtracts the loss twice. **Impact:** false equity, leverage/cushion, peak/drawdown, rank gates, EOD score and margin liquidation; high-stakes state persists into saves. **Fix/test:** use cash + long market value - short market value, leaving unrealized P&L as a separate display metric. Table-test long/short up/down, partial close, commission and margin threshold; migrate only if any stored derived fields need recalculation, never overwrite saved cash or positions.

### G2 · P0 · Pending buys bypass available-cash and margin limits

**Where:** `src/engine/market.ts:238-280,351-367,392-415`; `src/engine/portfolio.ts:173-190,191-246`.
**Reproduce (executed):** `new MarketSimulationEngine('reservation',1000)`; place two `buy limit` HLVG orders, five shares each, limit $1,000; both return `success: true`. Call `tick(0.05)`. **Expected:** reserve buying power at entry or recheck against the actual fill and reject/cancel the unaffordable remainder. **Actual:** both fill, 10 shares held and cash **-$520.30** although margin is locked. Buy checks run only at placement, when neither order reserves capital; `executeFill` does not check cash. **Impact:** free leverage, overnight interest and invalid risk/score. **Fix/test:** one shared pre-fill admission against spendable funds/existing reservations, accounting for commission and slippage; atomic when multiple orders trigger in a tick. Test two concurrent pending buys, jumps above estimated price, DAY/GTC cancellation releasing reservations, and bracket children. Do not apply a check that prevents buying to cover an existing short.

### G3 · P0 · A long can flip into a locked short

**Where:** `src/engine/portfolio.ts:175-181,248-313`; `src/engine/market.ts:219-280,392-415`; ticket locks SELL at `src/features/ticket/OrderTicket.tsx:38-41,171-185`.
**Reproduce (executed):** `new PortfolioEngine(10000)` (shorting locked); buy 10 HLVG; sell 11. **Expected:** reject the oversize sell before cash/position mutation, or fill only the 10 owned shares if explicit partial-fill semantics are implemented. **Actual:** `executeFill` reports success and the position becomes **-1 share**; permission checks only apply when *no* existing position exists. The rank-1 ticket shows a visible disabled `SELL (LOCKED)` button, but bracket sell children can still reach this path. **Impact:** unauthorized shorts and unexpected financing/losses, including through automatic exits. **Fix/test:** check projected position sign at the shared fill boundary before charging commission or mutating holdings; verify oversize manual and bracket sells, exact close, allowed flips at unlocked rank, and no side effects on rejection.

### G4 · P0 · Career progression beyond intern is unreachable

**Where:** `src/engine/career.ts:38-69,126-135,183-205,224-259`; only engine achievement call `src/engine/market.ts:382-389,418-422`. Repo-wide search found **no caller** of `addReputation` or `recordRiskBreach`; objectives are initialized once and never completed/rotated.
**Reproduce (code-traced):** start a default career; trade and/or advance days until equity clears $135,000; `checkRankUp` additionally requires 100 reputation (`src/engine/career.ts:51-57,183-191`) but reputation remains zero throughout ordinary engine actions. **Expected:** documented objective/reputation awards permit rank 2 and later unlocks. **Actual:** rank stays at intern, even if the equity requirement is met; margin/short feature unlock path cannot run. **Impact:** career, most achievements and advanced mechanics cannot be earned normally. **Fix/test:** award and persist reputation on defined objective events, enforce/communicate rank requirements, and test a deterministic multi-day advancement path plus export/import. Test risk-breach progression when enforcement is wired, without retroactively invalidating existing saves.

### G5 · P1 · Bracket exit orders are not linked or one-cancels-other

**Where:** `src/engine/market.ts:392-452`; `src/engine/matching.ts:27-31`; `src/engine/portfolio.ts:274-313`.
**Reproduce (executed placement, code-traced completion):** submit a one-share market buy with take-profit and stop-loss. The resulting snapshot contains **two** independent pending sells (`limit`, `stop`), each quantity 1, with no `parentOrderId` or `isBracketChild`. Trigger take-profit, then later stop-loss. **Expected:** the first filled exit cancels the other; after the initial position closes neither remains executable. **Actual:** `evaluateOrder` deletes only the filled order; the second remains and can sell again, opening a short if unlocked. If locked, the rejected fill stays pending and repeatedly retries. **Impact:** involuntary reversal, stale risk protection and misleading order state. **Fix/test:** link children using the existing optional `parentOrderId`/`isBracketChild` fields (`src/saves/schema.ts:205-209`), then cancel siblings on fill, manual close or parent cancellation; test both trigger orders, save/reload and partial exits. Add a save migration only if the existing fields cannot express the chosen linkage. Disable or explicitly mark unsafe brackets until OCO works.

### G6 · P1 · Day report displays lifetime, fabricated and pre-fee figures as daily results

**Where:** `src/engine/market.ts:455-525`; `src/engine/portfolio.ts:68-80,83-95,346-365`; rendered in `src/features/eod-report/EndOfDayModal.tsx:14-52`.
**Reproduce (executed):** `new MarketSimulationEngine('audit-repro',10000)`; `skipToNextDay()`. Report has `startingEquity: 100000` and `netPnl` around **-$90,000** for an account started at $10,000. **Expected:** day-start equity from portfolio, end equity *after* overnight charges, net = end - start; actual per-sector P&L or no attribution. **Actual:** hard-coded $100,000 lifetime baseline, risk sampled before financing, attribution assigned `exposure * 0.02` even on flat/losing days. Trades are selected by last wall-clock hour, not sim day (`:484-485`); zero P&L is mishandled by `|| Infinity` (`:491-492`). **Impact:** misleading coach, daily performance, sector attribution and scenario grade. **Fix/test:** snapshot true day-start, mark current prices, apply fees then compute end metrics; associate fills with sim day, calculate real contributions or omit attribution; test non-default starting capital, consecutive days, zero-profit trades and financing.

### G7 · P1 · Candle time and volume use wall clock and fictitious shares

**Where:** `src/engine/market.ts:294-349,520-525`; `src/engine/pricing.ts:334-405`; chart `src/features/chart/TradingViewChart.tsx:157-179,219-231`.
**Reproduce (code-traced, one tick executed):** advance several simulated minutes or call `skipToNextDay()` repeatedly within one real minute; read `exportState().pricing.states[0].candles1m.at(-1)` and `candles1D.at(-1)`. **Expected:** new sim-time minute/day candles and each bucket's *incremental* traded/simulated volume. **Actual:** `Date.now()` supplies bucket time while `simSecondsOfDay` advances independently (one tick advanced 3.25 sim seconds); `accumulateCandle` starts new bars at **volume 0** and adds a constant **100** per subsequent tick, ignoring `volume`. Several sim days collapse into one wall-clock 1D candle; chart VWAP/volume histograms consume these bars. **Impact:** price history, technical indicators and deterministic full replay are unreliable at 5x/20x or after reload. **Fix/test:** persist a monotonic sim epoch with day/session rollover; aggregate positive per-tick volume delta per timeframe and ensure all series share ascending unique time. Test accelerated days, pause/reload, day rollover and per-timeframe volume totals ([TradingView's series data/update semantics](https://tradingview.github.io/lightweight-charts/docs#updating-the-data-in-a-series)).

### G8 · P1 · Stop-limit stops are forgotten after the trigger tick

**Where:** `src/engine/matching.ts:9-32,130-150`; persistence `src/engine/market.ts:159-205`.
**Reproduce (executed matcher):** pending BUY with stop $105 and limit $106; evaluate at `currentPrice=107, bestAsk=107` (stop crossed but too expensive), then at `currentPrice=104, bestAsk=105`. **Expected:** stop latches into a resting buy limit, then fills at $105 even after spot falls back below $105. **Actual:** `null`, `null`; no triggered state exists and each tick retests the stop condition. **Impact:** risk/entry intent silently missed. **Fix/test:** persist triggered status on the order, evaluate subsequent ticks as a limit, test trigger without fill, subsequent return, cancellation and restore across save/reload.

### G9 · P1 · Limit fills ignore displayed depth for large orders

**Where:** `src/engine/matching.ts:90-105`; compare book walk `src/engine/orderbook.ts:112-159` and estimate `src/features/ticket/OrderTicket.tsx:44-83`.
**Reproduce (code-traced):** place a buy limit for more than `depth.asks[0].size` at a limit above the best ask. **Expected:** only shares available up to the limit fill, at corresponding depth levels, or remaining shares stay pending; never fill beyond displayed liquidity at a single best-ask price. **Actual:** every share fills immediately at best ask, regardless of available size. Ticket estimates a multi-level walk while actual execution does not. **Impact:** artificially cheap large orders and conflicting previews. **Fix/test:** share one depth-walk pricing/fill policy between preview and execution; test level exhaustion, limit cap, remaining shares and commission for partial fills. If depth is decorative, remove misleading execution claims instead of simulating unlimited liquidity.

### G10 · P1 · Scenario regime, duration and scoring rules are not enforced

**Where:** `src/data/scenarios.ts:3-65`, `src/store/useTerminalStore.ts:374-380`; pricing regime setter `src/engine/pricing.ts` (`setRegime`).
**Reproduce (code-traced):** start `rate_shock_autumn`, whose `initialRegime` is `bear`, `durationDays` is 10 and `targetProfit` is $15,000; inspect the engine's initial regime and advance past day 10. **Expected:** promised initial market regime and a 10-day scored challenge. **Actual:** `startScenario` applies the scenario seed and featured ticker, but does not apply its initial regime or evaluate duration/target/benchmark. **Impact:** players receive a named challenge whose scoring and initial market condition are not enacted. **Fix/test:** apply scenario rules explicitly and persist progress/end-state, or relabel them as seed presets; test start, day-10 evaluation and restore. Avoid changing existing careers without an opt-in/migration.

### G11 · P2 · Scheduled catalysts and macro headlines do not match their claimed market effects

**Where:** `src/engine/market.ts:141-148,323-345,466-473`; `src/engine/events.ts:97-117`; `src/engine/correlation.ts` (regime configurations).
**Reproduce (code-traced):** run beyond sim day 46; `upcomingEarnings` is generated only for days 2-46. On generated macro/black-swan news with no ticker, `tick` posts the headline but queues a jump only when `news.ticker` is truthy. **Expected:** future catalyst calendar is replenished and indicated sector/macro shocks move relevant prices. **Actual:** earnings stop after the initial horizon; untargeted news is text only. **Impact:** long careers lose catalysts and players can misread news as a trading signal. **Fix/test:** roll the earnings calendar on a bounded cadence, consume sector/global impacts in pricing (or label headlines decorative); assert post-day-46 events and same-seed event effects.

### G12 · P2 · Orders trade outside the advertised regular session

**Where:** `src/engine/market.ts:219-280,302-317,351-367`; `src/features/ticket/OrderTicket.tsx:209-220` labels DAY as session-bound.
**Reproduce (code-traced):** advance past 16:00 while before 16:30, then submit a market order or leave a pending limit. **Expected:** state an explicit extended-hours policy and hold regular-session orders until reopening, or apply extended-hours spread/risk rules. **Actual:** market order evaluates immediately; pending orders evaluate every tick regardless of `session`. **Impact:** DAY orders fill after the trading day the player sees; model never accounts for extended-hours liquidity. **Fix/test:** define an explicit session policy in engine, UI and save semantics, then test boundary times 16:00, 16:30 and next 09:30.

## Opportunity list (not claimed as defects)

- **Market calibration:** current correlated shocks, regime changes, GARCH-ish conditional variance and five-level synthetic liquidity are a coherent simulator foundation (`src/engine/pricing.ts:194-331`, `src/engine/correlation.ts`, `src/engine/orderbook.ts:39-110`). After correctness fixes, calibrate spread/volume/volatility jointly from a documented target, rather than stacking arbitrary noise. `oneDayParametricVaR` uses a fixed annual 20% volatility (`src/engine/portfolio.ts:451-455`); label it a baseline estimate until validated.
- **Rivals and pedagogy:** rivals update daily by seeded style-dependent drift (`src/engine/rivals.ts:169-226`), not trades in the same market. Make their holdings and performance causally consistent if head-to-head strategy training becomes a goal; otherwise call them simulated benchmarks. Progression objectives need their own small, testable event inputs rather than a second simulation loop.
- **Replay readiness:** replace wall-clock IDs/timestamps (`src/engine/market.ts:253-269,325,348,405`, `src/engine/portfolio.ts:325-340`) only if trusted replay/global leaderboard moves forward. Preserve separate human-readable wall time for UI; do not treat current client `actionSeq` or saved scores as verified (see `docs/ROADMAP.md:34-35,57`). The existing 48-symbol/main-thread design need not be rewritten to fix these issues.
